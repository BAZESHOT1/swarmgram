## Kinto 1.10.75

Design update for phones and desktop.

- Chat screen redesigned: glass back button, name and status capsule, square avatar with a status ring, round attach button, glass message field and microphone, translucent message bubbles.
- Chat backgrounds: a default Kinto pattern (diagonal stripes and grid) or any photo you choose. In direct chats any participant can change the background, and the chat gets a system notice "<name> changed the chat background". In groups and channels the owner and admins can (moderators and members only if the owner enables the new "Change background" permission).
- Profile redesigned: background photo chosen by the owner, large square avatar with name, role and online status, pinned channel card, info card (username, about, birthday, location), activity card (wearables, repeaters, gateways and approximate coverage area), tabs Gifts / Stories / Modules with the user's modules.
- Profile editing: profile photo (as before), new profile background and a choice of the channel or group shown on the profile.
- Group and channel info: new "Background" block. Manage tab: new "Change background" permission.
- Same layouts for the desktop/web version.

Hub 1.4.27: background and featured-chat endpoints, new permission, system notice for direct chats (migration 048).
