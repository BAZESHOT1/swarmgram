## Kinto 1.10.81

- Pretty chat links on the web: direct chats open as `/chat/@username` (and `/chat/@username/info`); the service `?__EXPO_ROUTER_key=…` parameter is removed from the address bar.
- Chat header fixed: the name capsule is centred and stretches between the back button and the photo, which sits at the right edge.
- Group and channel info: new "Invite" action with a menu — copy a permanent invite link (any member of a public group/channel can share it) or add members. "Add members" lists people you have chats with plus search across all users; users whose privacy settings forbid invites are shown greyed out with a lock and a notice.
- Voice messages match the glass bubbles: transparent background, accent play button, waveform that fills as it plays, elapsed time; they now play on iOS and Android too (previously web only).
- Settings → Appearance → Style: "Glass" (default) or "Monotone" (no translucency: solid grey / off-white buttons, panels and classic message bubbles). Saved to the account like the theme.

Hub 1.4.31 (migration 050: UI style, permanent invite links; share-link and invite-candidates endpoints).
